# ⚡ Debate Me – AI vs You

**A fun, educational debate game where you argue against an AI opponent and get scored on logic and rhetoric.**

## 🧠 About

Pick a controversial topic, state your argument, and the AI will respond with a prepared counter‑argument. Your performance is scored in real‑time based on:

- **Logic** – use of reasoning, evidence, and conditional statements.
- **Rhetoric** – persuasive language, strong phrasing, and rhetorical questions.

The app is perfect for schools, debate clubs, or just sharpening your own argumentation skills. Share your high scores on Twitter!

## ✨ Features

- **4 Controversial Topics** – AI & future, Climate action, Privacy vs security, Education reform.
- **AI Opponent** – Responds with curated counter‑arguments that cycle to keep the debate fresh.
- **Scoring System** – Instant feedback on logic and rhetoric (max 30 points each).
- **Responsive UI** – Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- **Keyboard Shortcut** – Press `Enter` to submit your argument.
- **Reset & Switch Topics** – Change topics anytime to start a new debate.
